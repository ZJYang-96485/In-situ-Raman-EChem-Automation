EARLY-STAGE PROJECT SUMMARY

Under construction

This I-Corps introduction captures working hypotheses only. Customer-discovery evidence and project decisions will be added here as they are validated.

A reproducible workflow for operando Raman electrochemistry

SpectraLoop is being developed to organize electrochemical configuration, Raman-acquisition planning, Raman-owned preprocessing, and traceable post-experimental analysis in one laboratory workflow.

PROBLEM HYPOTHESIS

Fragmented instrument workflows

Operando studies can require separate control software, manual timing alignment, and disconnected data handoffs. The resulting workflow may make repeatability and interpretation harder than necessary.

INITIAL USERS

Researchers and shared facilities

Discovery will examine the needs of electrochemistry researchers, Raman users, facility staff, and laboratory instrumentation leads. These groups are hypotheses, not validated market segments.

PROPOSED VALUE

Traceable experimental context

The intended platform records model-specific instrument settings, acquisition plans, preprocessing provenance, and analysis context so a dataset carries its experimental history forward.

CUSTOMER-DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

Questions to test before defining a product

  • Where do users lose time or confidence when coordinating electrochemistry and Raman measurements?
  • Which configuration, timing, and preprocessing records are essential for reproducible work?
  • How should a local, safety-gated instrument workflow fit with existing facility practices and software?
